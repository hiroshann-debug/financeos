1. What We Collect

1. අපි එකතු කරන දේ

We collect only what's necessary to provide the Service:

2. How We Use Your Data

2. ඔබේ දත්ත භාවිතය
  • Provide the service — display your dashboard, calculate metrics, show AI advice
  • Personalisation — remember your preferred name, currency, theme
  • AI Advisor — send your financial summary to Claude API to generate responses
  • Security — detect and prevent unauthorised access
  • Improvements — fix bugs and improve features (aggregated, not individual)

We do NOT use your data for: advertising, selling to third parties, profiling, or training AI models.

3. How We Store Your Data

3. දත්ත ගබඩා කිරීම
  • Database: PostgreSQL via Supabase — encrypted at rest, hosted on AWS
  • Authentication: Auth0 — industry-standard OAuth 2.0, no passwords stored by us
  • Hosting: Railway — encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Retention: Your data is kept until you delete your account
  • Backups: Supabase maintains automated backups

Each user's data is completely isolated — it is technically impossible for one user to access another user's data.

5. AI Advisor & Privacy

5. AI උපදේශකය සහ පෞද්ගලිකත්වය

When you use the AI Advisor, we send a summary of your financial data to Anthropic's Claude API. This summary includes:

  • Wallet balances and total cash
  • Income and expense totals for the current period
  • Top spending categories
  • Credit card utilization and due dates
  • Loan outstanding balances
  • Savings goal progress

Important: Anthropic does not store API conversations or use them to train models. See Anthropic's Privacy Policy for details. Your name and email are never sent to the AI.

7. Cookies

7. කුකීස්
